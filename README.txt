CELLULAR AUTOMATA HERO UPDATE

The hero background now runs Conway's Game of Life rules:
- A live cell survives with 2 or 3 live neighbors.
- A dead cell becomes alive with exactly 3 live neighbors.
- Hovering kills nearby cells.
- Clicking adds a charged living cluster.
- Fully charged cells retain the glow effect.
- Very low or stalled populations are lightly reseeded so the background keeps moving.
